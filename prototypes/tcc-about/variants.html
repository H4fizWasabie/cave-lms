About us

A Malaysian professional training and consultancy company

The Cave Consult (TCC) is a Malaysian professional training and consultancy company, registered as an HRD Corp Training Provider and with the Ministry of Finance Malaysia (MOF).

TCC specialises in professional competency development, leadership, artificial intelligence, digital transformation, strategic management and sustainability. The company serves government agencies, Government-Linked Companies (GLCs), educational institutions and private sector organisations through practical, industry-focused training and consultancy solutions.

TCC is led by Coach Adam Daniel, Strategic Intelligence Architect and Founder & Chief Consultant of The Cave Consult, a Certified NLP Trainer and Hypnotherapy Certified Trainer with over 25 years of experience in entrepreneurship, business management and leadership. Coach Adam\'s profile

Together with The Cave Consult Team, TCC combines professional expertise, industry experience and practical training methodologies to deliver competency-based learning solutions that address evolving workplace challenges.

Through its professional training programmes, consultancy services and digital learning initiatives, including CAVELMS, The Cave Consult is committed to promoting Lifelong Learning for Efficiency & Career Development, enabling individuals and organisations to strengthen professional competencies, improve workplace productivity and support sustainable career growth.
